TRICYCLE RIDES · ALBAY

Quick, local and almost everywhere.

Motorized tricycles are one of the most useful ways to get around towns and barangays in the Philippines. They are perfect for short trips when walking is too far and a car is unnecessary.

Great for short local tripsEasy to find in townsA very Filipino way to travel

WHAT IS A TRICYCLE?

A motorcycle and sidecar built for everyday life.

A Philippine tricycle is normally a motorcycle fitted with a covered sidecar. Designs differ from place to place, and passenger capacity depends on the vehicle and local rules.

In Albay, tricycles and motorcycles are documented as normal public transportation in local communities. They are especially useful for markets, short town trips and roads not served conveniently by larger public transport.

Unlike jeepneys, a tricycle trip may be direct to your destination. Agree on the fare before leaving if there is no clearly posted tariff.

PRICE EXPECTATIONS

Local fares vary by town, distance and whether the ride is private.

There is no single nationwide tricycle price that we should pretend applies everywhere. For tourists, the safest approach is to ask the local fare before the ride. The figures below are practical planning estimates only.
