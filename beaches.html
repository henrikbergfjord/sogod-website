BEFORE YOU GO

Island days are better when you keep them flexible.

✓ Bring drinking water, sunscreen and practical footwear.

✓ Use dry bags or waterproof protection for phones and valuables.

✓ Confirm life jackets and passenger capacity with the boat operator.

✓ Weather and sea conditions always take priority over the original plan.

✓ Some island locations have few or no shops, toilets or facilities.

Destination information is based on Province of Albay and Malilipot tourism information. Local conditions, fees and accessibility can change.
