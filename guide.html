Ride the volcanic landscape around Mayon.

ATV operators around Cagsawa and Legazpi offer everything from short beginner rides to longer lava trails. The rates below are current published reference rates from local operators and should always be reconfirmed before booking.

Trail Approx. time Reference price Best for
Grassland Trail 20–30 min ₱500 First-time riders

Combo Trail 50–60 min ₱1,250 More variety
Green Lava Trail 2–2.5 hours ₱1,850 Classic Mayon experience
Black Lava Trail 2–3 hours ₱2,250 Rougher volcanic terrain
2018 Mayon Lava Trail 2–3 hours ₱2,600 Longer lava adventure
Mayon ATV Summit route 4–5 hours from ₱5,399 Full adventure day
🌤️

Go early

Mayon's summit is often clearer in the morning before clouds build.

👟
